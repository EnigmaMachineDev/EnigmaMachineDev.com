Ambitus

Every way to play the chord, rated for your hand. Give Ambitus a tuning, how many strings the chord uses, a fret window, and the scale degrees you want — it enumerates every fingering that fits, rates each one for playability with a real finger-assignment solver, and groups them so the comfortable voicings rise to the top. Fully offline, no account, no tracking.

Coming to Google Play
Features

What Ambitus does

🎸

Every Voicing, Enumerated

Set the parameters — tuning, string count, fret window, whether string skips are allowed, which degrees you want — and Ambitus finds every fingering that satisfies them. Not a lookup table of the usual shapes.

🖐️

Rated for Real Hands

A genuine finger-assignment solver, not a rule of thumb. It enforces finger order and barre legality to tell you what's comfortable, what's a stretch, and what's simply impossible — and you choose how much difficulty to see.

↔️

Shapes, Not Positions

Results are fingerings, normalized so they're slidable anywhere on the neck. Learn a shape once and it works at every root — names, degrees, and pitches are worked out from wherever you put it.

🔁

Tuning Equivalence

Only the gaps between strings matter, so EADG and BEAD are the same shape space — and Ambitus says so. Favorites are keyed to the gaps, so they dedupe across equivalent tunings and never orphan when you rename one.

🔍

Identify & Scales

Tap notes onto a fretboard and get the chord read back as ranked interpretations, ambiguity included. Or browse scale box coverings to see where a degree set sits on the neck.

🔒

Yours & Private

Everything stays on your device — no account, no network, no analytics. Your tunings, chord definitions, and favorites export to a single file that the FretboardTools website reads too, so you can favorite shapes at the desk and recall them with the bass in hand.
