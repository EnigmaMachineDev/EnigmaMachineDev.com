Android · Randomizer

Auspex

Build your own randomizer as a graph of connected pieces. Option lists, number ranges, dice, and card decks are tiles you wire together — and you wire them by outcome, so landing on one result fires the next roll while a different result goes somewhere else entirely. One tap cascades through the whole chain until every branch runs out.

Features

What Auspex does

🧩

Five Kinds of Tile

Weighted option lists, number ranges, dice, card decks that draw without replacement, and fixed constants. Mix them freely — a single randomizer can roll a d20, pick from a list, and deal a card.

🔀

Wired by Outcome

Connections hang off specific results, not whole tiles. Landing on "Italian" can fire the pasta list while "Thai" goes somewhere else, and a number lands in a range that routes it — so the roll takes a different route every time.

♻️

Reuse Anything

One tile can be wired in from as many places as you like — build a difficulty roll once and point everything at it. Loops are allowed too, and Auspex handles them without spinning out.

🕸️

Animated Decision Graph

See the whole randomizer as a graph — one box per tile, scaled to fit your screen on open, with pinch-zoom, pan, and a side-on or top-down toggle. Roll it and the path lights up a layer at a time, each tile showing what it picked.

📜

Full Roll History

Every roll is recorded as the complete tree of what fired what, not just a final answer. Open any past roll and replay it across the graph to see exactly how it got there.

🔒

Yours & Private

Everything stays on your device — no account, no network, no tracking. Share a single randomizer as one file (it imports as an independent copy, so re-importing never overwrites yours), or back up everything at once.
