Android · Reading & Read-Aloud

Lector

A reading library that can also read to you. Bring in a book a chapter at a time — pasted, or from a PDF, EPUB or Word file — read it with the layout you like, and when your eyes need a rest, let your phone read it aloud and pick up exactly where you left off. Nothing leaves your device.

Features

What Lector does

📥

Bring Your Own Text

Paste it, or open TXT, Markdown, HTML, PDF, EPUB, DOCX, ODT or RTF files. Lector cleans up broken lines, split words and PDF page headers, proposes chapter breaks, and lets you adjust every split before saving.

📚

Build A Book A Chapter At A Time

Start a work with Chapter 1 and append Chapter 2 when it arrives — at the end or anywhere in between. Organise works into collections, give them covers, and split, merge or edit sections later without losing your place.

📖

A Reader Set Up Your Way

Scroll or turn pages, with themes, fonts (including a dyslexia-friendly one), size, spacing, margins and alignment. Search, a table of contents, bookmarks, and highlights with notes are all built in.

🔊

Read-Aloud That Keeps Going

Your phone's own text-to-speech voice reads on with the screen off, with lock-screen and headset controls, follow-along highlighting, a sleep timer, speed and voice choice, and pronunciation rules for the names it gets wrong.

🔖

One Place, By Eye Or Ear

Every work keeps a single saved position shared by reading and listening. Stop the voice on the bus, open the book at home, and you're on the same sentence.

🔒

Yours & Private

No account, no sync, no analytics, no cloud voices, and no internet permission. PDF reading happens entirely on your device. Your library exports whole or one work at a time as readable JSON, or as plain text.
