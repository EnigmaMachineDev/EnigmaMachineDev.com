Lector

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 App: Lector (Android) Developer: EnigmaMachineDev

Overview

Lector is an offline Android application. It makes no network requests of any kind — there is no account, no sync, no cloud voice service, no font or metadata lookup, and no third-party API. It does not collect personal information and does not transmit any data to external servers. Everything you bring into the app stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Lector does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a private application folder:

  • The text you add — pasted, or extracted from files you open — organized into works and sections
  • Cover images you choose for your works
  • Your reading position in each work, which sections you have finished, and reading and listening time
  • Bookmarks, highlights, and the notes you attach to them
  • Collections, pronunciation rules, and your reader and voice settings

None of this data is linked to your identity, uploaded anywhere, or shared with any third party.

Files You Open

When you add text from a file, Lector reads only the file you picked through Android's system file picker. The text is extracted on your device — PDFs are read by a copy of pdf.js bundled inside the app, not by any online service — and the original file is not kept or modified. Lector cannot open files you have not chosen, and it does not scan your storage.

Read-Aloud

Read-aloud uses the text-to-speech engine installed on your device, chosen in your Android settings. Lector hands that engine the sentences being read and nothing else; it does not use any cloud voice. The speech engine is a separate app provided by your device maker or another developer, and how it handles text is governed by its own privacy policy, not ours. While read-aloud is playing, Lector shows a media notification with playback controls so it can keep reading with the screen off.

How We Use Your Data

Data stored by Lector is used solely to operate the app's features on your device:

  • Displaying your works in the reader and reading them aloud
  • Remembering your place in each work, shared between reading and listening
  • Showing your bookmarks, highlights, notes, and search results
  • Restoring app state between sessions

Export & Import

Lector includes optional backup and export features. You can export your whole library or a single work — text, covers, positions, and annotations included — as a JSON file, or a work as plain text, through Android's standard share sheet. You can import a Lector export through Android's system file picker, either merging with your library or replacing it. Neither process routes data through any server — all file operations are local and on-device. You choose what you do with exported files, and where they go.

Permissions Required

Lector requests only what its features need:

  • Notifications — requested the first time you start read-aloud, so the playback controls can appear in the notification shade and on the lock screen
  • Foreground service (media playback) and wake lock — so read-aloud keeps going while the screen is off or you are in another app, and so the screen can stay on while you read if you turn that setting on
  • Photo picker — only when you choose an image from your library to use as a cover
  • Storage Access Framework — used to open files you pick and to write files you asked to export. Lector never browses or reads storage on its own.

Lector does not request the camera, the microphone, your contacts, or your location, and it holds no internet permission at all — the released app is built without it, so it is not merely unused but absent.

Data Storage & Security

All app data, including the text of your works and their covers, is stored in a private application folder that belongs to Lector and is not accessible to other apps. This data is removed when you uninstall Lector. If you have Android's built-in backup enabled, your device OS may back up app data to your Google account; this is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours.

Data Sharing

Lector does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or external APIs integrated into the app.

Deleting Your Data

You can erase all data stored by Lector at any time from within the app (Settings → Data → Delete all data), by clearing the app's data in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Lector → Storage → Clear Data), or by uninstalling the app.

Your Content

Lector does not supply, sell, or check any text. You are responsible for having the right to use the material you bring into the app. Lector cannot open DRM-protected books.

Children's Privacy

Lector does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of Lector after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.

Contact
