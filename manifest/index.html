Manifest

Know what you own, where it lives, and grab everything an activity needs in one trip. Map your space into a browsable tree of locations, fill containers, and place items — boxed or standing on their own. Start an activity for a room-by-room retrieval plan, check gear out while you use it, and put it all back home in one pass so the map never goes stale.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy
Features

What Manifest does

🗺️

Map Your Space

Build a browsable tree of locations — House › Garage › Shelf A — as deep as you like. Every location can hold containers and items placed directly at it.

🏷️

Every Thing, Placed

An item lives in a container or stands on its own at a location. A lawnmower in the garage is exactly as normal as a screwdriver in a bin — both are first-class.

🧾

Grab It In One Trip

Save an activity as a list of things you need, then start it for a retrieval plan grouped by location and container — with anything missing flagged up top.

↩️

Keep The Map Honest

Check items out while you use them and the Put Away tab tracks what's still out. Put it all back home in one pass — returning is instant, since home never moved.

🔳

Printable QR Labels

Print a manifest card for any container — name, size, location, and contents — with a QR code that opens the app straight to it. One label per page or an Avery-style sheet.

🔒

Yours & Private

Everything stays on your device — no account, no network, no tracking. Export a single-file backup (photos included) to move or restore your whole inventory.
