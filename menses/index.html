Android · Cycle Tracker

Menses

A cycle tracker that keeps your cycle to itself. Log the days a period starts and ends, and Menses works out your averages, predicts what comes next, and shows where you are in your cycle today — with an optional daily log when you want more detail. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your phone.

Features

What Menses does

🩸

Two Taps Is Enough

Mark the day a period starts and the day it ends — that's all Menses needs. Cycle lengths, period lengths and averages are worked out from those dates; nothing else is ever required.

📅

Predictions With Honest Ranges

The next three periods are projected from your own recent cycles, shown as a range that reflects how regular you actually are. Until there's enough history, it uses the lengths you set and says so. Running late? It keeps counting and simply says "expected any day".

🌙

Your Phase, Any Day

A cycle ring and colour-coded calendar show the menstrual, follicular, ovulatory and luteal phases for any day, past or ahead. The fertile window is shown too — or hidden entirely with one switch.

📝

An Optional Daily Log

Note flow, symptoms, mood and a few words on any day you like, with your own custom symptoms alongside the built-in ones. It never feeds the predictions, and you can hide it from the app completely.

📊

Insights From Your Own History

Cycle and period lengths over time, how each cycle split across its phases, which symptoms tend to show up in which phase, and how flow changes across a period — all drawn from what you logged.

🔒

Discreet & Private

No account, no sync, no analytics, and no internet permission. Reminders are off until you turn them on, and by default they say only "Reminder from Menses" — never a word about your cycle on the lock screen.
