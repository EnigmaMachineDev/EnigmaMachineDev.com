Menses

Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 8, 2026 App: Menses (Android) Developer: EnigmaMachineDev

Overview

Menses is an offline Android application. It makes no network requests of any kind — there is no account, no sync, no cloud backup service, and no third-party API. It does not collect personal information and does not transmit any data to external servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Menses does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a private application folder:

  • The dates your periods started and ended
  • Daily log entries, if you choose to make them — flow, symptoms, mood, and any notes you write
  • Any custom symptoms you define
  • Your settings — default cycle and period lengths, display choices, and reminder preferences

Cycle history, averages, predictions, and phases are calculated from those dates each time the app opens and are not stored separately. None of this data is linked to your identity, uploaded anywhere, or shared with any third party.

Reproductive Health Data

The period dates and daily logs you record in Menses are sensitive health data, and they are treated accordingly: they are stored only on your device, they are never transmitted anywhere, and they are not used for any purpose other than displaying them back to you. There is no server that holds a copy, so there is nothing that could be requested from us, sold, or exposed in a breach. Menses does not read from or write to Google Fit, Health Connect, or any other health platform, and requests no permission to do so.

How We Use Your Data

Data stored by Menses is used solely to operate the app's features on your device:

  • Working out your cycle and period lengths and their averages
  • Estimating upcoming periods, cycle phases, and — if you leave it switched on — the fertile window
  • Producing the charts and summaries on the Insights screen
  • Scheduling the reminders you switch on
  • Restoring app state between sessions

Notifications

Menses can schedule local reminders — before a predicted period, on the day it is due, once if a period is running late, and before the fertile window. Every reminder is off until you turn it on, and Menses asks for notification permission only at that moment, never at launch. Reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device by Android's notification system; no remote push service is used. By default they use discreet wording ("Reminder from Menses") that says nothing about your cycle, so nothing sensitive appears on your lock screen unless you choose otherwise.

Export & Import

Menses includes an optional backup feature. You can export all of your data as a single JSON file through Android's standard share sheet, and import a Menses export through Android's system file picker, either replacing or merging with what is already in the app. An exported file is plain, readable text, and the app reminds you of that before it is shared. Neither process routes data through any server — all file operations are local and on-device. You choose what you do with exported files, and where they go.

Permissions Required

Menses requests only the following:

  • Notifications — only if you switch on a reminder
  • Storage Access Framework — used for import and export through the system file picker and share sheet. Menses uses it solely to read a file you pick and to write a file you asked to export, and never browses or reads storage on its own.

Menses does not request the camera, your contacts, your location, or the microphone, and it holds no internet permission — the released app is built without it, so it is not merely unused but absent.

Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored in a private application folder that belongs to Menses and is not accessible to other apps. This data is removed when you uninstall Menses. If you have Android's built-in backup enabled, your device OS may back up app data to your Google account; this is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours. If you would rather your cycle data never leave the phone in any form, you can turn off Android's app backup in your device settings.

Data Sharing

Menses does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or external APIs integrated into the app.

Deleting Your Data

You can erase all data stored by Menses at any time from within the app (Settings → Data → Delete all data), by clearing the app's data in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Menses → Storage → Clear Data), or by uninstalling the app.

Not Medical Advice Or Contraception

Menses records the dates you give it and reports estimates calculated from them. Predicted periods, phases, and fertile windows are averages, not measurements, and cycles vary. Menses is not a medical device, makes no health claims, and must not be used as a method of contraception or to plan or prevent pregnancy. It is not a substitute for advice from a qualified healthcare professional.

Children's Privacy

Menses does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.
