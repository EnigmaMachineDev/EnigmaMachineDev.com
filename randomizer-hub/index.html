Features

What RandomizerTools does

🎮

Game Randomizers

Character and loadout rolls for Elden Ring, Dark Souls, Skyrim, Warframe, Minecraft, Civ, and dozens more — turn a familiar game into a fresh run.

🍽️

Food & Activity

Order pickers for the chains you actually eat at, plus meals, date ideas, travel, workouts, and hobbies for when nobody can agree. Stop arguing, start spinning.

🛠️

Build Your Own

Make a randomizer out of your own lists with the Build Your Own Randomizer tool — no code, and nothing to install.

🎲

Everyday Utilities

Beyond the randomizers: a dice roller, number picker, option picker, multi-timer, and sleep cycle and schedule calculators.

⚙️

Configurable Options

Toggle categories, exclude specific items, and save your preferences to localStorage so the randomizer remembers your setup.

🌐

No Install Required

Runs entirely in your browser. No downloads, no sign-up, no loading spinner — just open and spin.

📱

Works Everywhere

Fully responsive design works on desktop, tablet, and phone. Same experience no matter the screen size.

🔮

Growing Library

New randomizers are added regularly across different categories. Check back often or follow for updates.
