Regard

Remember the little things people tell you, so you can show you were listening. A favorite tea, a daughter's first week of college, a race they're training for: jot it down in seconds, find it later, and follow up. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your phone.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy
Features

What Regard does

✍️

Capture In Seconds

Tap, pick someone, type, save. A new person can be added without leaving the sheet, a category is suggested as you write, and a draft survives closing by accident.

👤

Everything About Someone, At A Glance

Open a person and see quick facts up top, then what you know grouped into likes, family, work, goals and more. Pin what you never want to forget, like “vegetarian” or “daughter Emma, 7”.

💬

Empty Your Head After A Date

Log a date, coffee or call, then list everything you learned in one pass. Each thing is filed under the right person, and it all shows on a timeline of your time together.

🔔

Follow-Ups And Birthdays

“In 1 week, ask Sam about the interview.” Set a follow-up for a date or a number of days away, snooze it if the timing's off, and get an optional yearly birthday reminder.

🎁

Gift Ideas Across Everyone

Flag anything someone mentions as a gift idea, and it collects in one place, grouped by person. Search every note at once to answer “who likes fly fishing?” when you need it.

🔒

Private By Design

No account, no sync, no analytics, no internet permission, and no access to your contacts, messages or location. Regard holds only what you type, and you can hide reminder text for a phone left face up.
