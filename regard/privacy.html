Regard

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026 App: Regard (Android) Developer: EnigmaMachineDev

Overview

Regard is an offline Android application: a private notebook for the things people tell you about themselves. It makes no network requests of any kind — there is no account, no sync, no cloud backup service, and no third-party API. It does not collect personal information and does not transmit any data to external servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Regard does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a private application folder, and only what you type or choose yourself:

  • People you add — a name, and if you choose to add them a nickname, relationship, how you met, birthday, and photo
  • Details you write down about them, with their category, date, and where you heard them
  • Interactions you log — the date, kind, place, who was there, and a summary
  • Follow-ups you set, with their dates and reminder times
  • Your own categories and circles
  • Your settings — reminder defaults, sort order, and display choices

None of this data is linked to your identity, uploaded anywhere, or shared with any third party.

Information About Other People

Regard is for remembering what people have chosen to tell you, and what you write about them can be personal. It is treated accordingly: it is stored only on your device, it is never transmitted anywhere, and it is not used for any purpose other than showing it back to you. There is no server that holds a copy, so there is nothing that could be requested from us, sold, or exposed in a breach. Regard never gathers anything on its own: it does not read your contacts, call log, messages, social media, clipboard, or location, and nothing appears in it that you did not put there. You are responsible for what you choose to write down, and for how you treat any file you export.

How We Use Your Data

Data stored by Regard is used solely to operate the app's features on your device:

  • Showing what you've noted about each person
  • Searching across your notes
  • Listing your follow-ups, upcoming birthdays, and gift ideas
  • Scheduling the reminders you set
  • Restoring app state between sessions

Notifications

Regard can schedule local reminders for a follow-up you have dated and, if you turn it on for someone, for their birthday each year. Regard asks for notification permission only the first time you set a reminder, never at launch. Reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device by Android's notification system; no remote push service is used. By default a reminder shows the person's name and what you wrote, so it can appear on your lock screen. A switch in Settings replaces that text with "You have a reminder in Regard".

Photos

You can add one photo per person. It is chosen through Android's system photo picker, which gives Regard only the image you select, so Regard needs no permission to read your photo library and cannot see anything else in it. A resized copy is stored in the app's private folder.

Export & Import

Regard includes an optional backup feature. You can export all of your data, with or without photos, as a single JSON file through Android's standard share sheet, and import a Regard export through Android's system file picker, which replaces what is in the app. Before each import, Regard saves a copy of your current data in its private folder, keeps the last three, and lets you restore one from Settings. An exported file is plain, readable text holding everything you've written about people, and the app says so before it is shared. Neither process routes data through any server — all file operations are local and on-device. You choose what you do with exported files, and where they go.

Permissions Required

Regard requests only the following:

  • Notifications — only when you first set a reminder
  • Storage Access Framework — used for import and export through the system file picker and share sheet. Regard uses it solely to read a file you pick and to write a file you asked to export, and never browses or reads storage on its own.

Regard does not request your contacts, call log, location, camera, microphone, or photo library, and it holds no internet permission — the released app is built without it, so it is not merely unused but absent.

Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored in a private application folder that belongs to Regard and is not accessible to other apps. This data is removed when you uninstall Regard. Regard turns off Android's app backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account or moved to a new phone automatically; the export feature is the way to keep a copy. Regard has no app lock, so anyone who can open your unlocked phone can open it.

Data Sharing

Regard does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or external APIs integrated into the app.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any person from within the app, which removes everything you noted about them, their follow-ups, and their photo. To erase everything, clear the app's data in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Regard → Storage → Clear Data) or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Regard does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.
