Android · Rule Tracker

Regula

Write rules for yourself, log when you break them, and see the pattern over time. On each day a rule is in effect it is either kept or broken, and Regula shows you how that adds up. No badges, no streak rewards, no pep talks, and nothing leaves your phone.

Features

What Regula does

📜

Rules With Their Reasons

Write anything you want to hold yourself to, and keep the reason beside it. A rule can be something to avoid or something to do, on every day or only the days you choose, with any number of categories.

✋

Log A Break In Two Taps

Tap Log break, tap Save. Add triggers and a note if you want them, or mark a break as excused so it stays in your history without counting against the rule. Days with nothing logged count as kept.

📈

See The Pattern

Adherence, clean runs, a calendar heatmap, and breaks by week, weekday and hour. Find out when you break a rule and why, across a week, a year, or everything, for one rule or a whole category.

🗂️

A Full History For Every Rule

Pause a rule for illness or travel without it counting for or against you. Retire it as kept, dropped or replaced, reinstate it later, and see every change on one timeline.

🔔

Reminders When They Matter

An optional reminder at the moment a rule applies: “No phone in bed” at 9:30 PM, with your reason underneath. Reminders are off until you set one, and they never nag about breaks.

🔒

Private By Design

No account, no sync, no analytics, no ads and no internet permission. Everything stays on your phone, with a full JSON backup and a CSV of your breaks when you want a copy.
