Regula

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 6, 2026 App: Regula (Android) Developer: EnigmaMachineDev

Overview

Regula is an offline Android application for writing rules for yourself and logging when you break them. It makes no network requests of any kind — there is no account, no sync, no cloud backup service, and no third-party API. It does not collect personal information and does not transmit any data to external servers. Everything you enter stays on your device.

Information We Collect

Regula does not collect any personal information. The app stores the following data locally on your device, in a private application folder, and only what you type or choose yourself:

  • Your rules — the title, your reason, notes, active days, start date, and reminder times
  • Each rule's history — when it was created, edited, paused, resumed, retired or reinstated, and any note you added
  • Breaks you log — the date and time, triggers, whether it was excused, and a note
  • Your own categories and triggers
  • Your settings — week start, day boundary, default chart range, and theme

None of this data is linked to your identity, uploaded anywhere, or shared with any third party.

How We Use Your Data

Data stored by Regula is used solely to operate the app's features on your device:

  • Working out which days each rule was kept or broken
  • Showing adherence, clean runs, charts and history
  • Scheduling the reminders you set
  • Restoring app state between sessions

Notifications

Regula can schedule a local reminder for a rule on the weekdays you choose. Reminders are off until you set one, and Regula asks for notification permission only the first time you turn one on, never at launch. Reminders are generated and delivered entirely on your device by Android's notification system; no remote push service is used. A reminder shows the rule's title and, if you wrote one, your reason, so what appears on your lock screen depends on your phone's lock screen settings. Keep that in mind if a rule is private.

Export & Import

Regula includes an optional backup feature. You can export all of your data as a single JSON file, or your breaks as a CSV file, through Android's standard share sheet, and import a Regula backup through Android's system file picker, either merging it with or replacing what is already in the app. An exported file is plain, readable text, and the app reminds you of that before a backup is shared. Neither process routes data through any server — all file operations are local and on-device. You choose what you do with exported files, and where they go.

Permissions

The only permission Regula ever asks you for is Notifications, and only when you first turn on a reminder. Import and export go through Android's system file picker and share sheet, which give Regula only the file you choose. Regula never browses or reads storage on its own.

Like other apps built with Expo and React Native, the installed app also declares permissions that come with those frameworks. Some support reminders, such as restarting them after your phone reboots and vibrating when one arrives. Others, such as storage access and drawing over other apps, are declared but never requested or used.

Regula does not request your contacts, location, camera, microphone, or photo library, and it holds no internet permission — the released app is built without it, so it is not merely unused but absent.

Data Storage & Security

All app data is stored in a private application folder that belongs to Regula and is not accessible to other apps. This data is removed when you uninstall Regula. If you have Android's built-in backup enabled, your device OS may back up app data to your Google account; this is governed by Google's privacy policy, not ours. If you would rather your data never leave the phone in any form, you can turn off Android's app backup in your device settings. Regula has no app lock, so anyone who can open your unlocked phone can open it.

Data Sharing

Regula does not share any data with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or external APIs integrated into the app.

Deleting Your Data

You can delete any rule from within the app, which removes its breaks and history, or erase everything at once with Delete all data in Settings. You can also clear the app's data in your Android settings (Settings → Apps → Regula → Storage → Clear Data) or uninstall the app.

Children's Privacy

Regula does not knowingly collect any information from children. The app collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.

Changes to This Policy

If this privacy policy changes, the updated version will be published at this URL with a revised effective date. Continued use of Regula after any changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
