Android · Vehicle Maintenance

Roadwrench

A maintenance and repair log for people who do their own work. Every oil change, brake job and fix becomes a dated, mileage-stamped record with photos of the receipt or the job — so when you sell the car, you hand over a service history instead of an 80,000-mile gap. No account, no cloud, nothing leaves your phone.

Features

What Roadwrench does

🔧

A Service History That Holds Up

Log what was done, when, at what mileage, by you or a shop — with parts, costs, notes and photos of receipts or the work itself. Back-dating is first-class, so an old paper log or spreadsheet can go in as-is.

🗓️

Schedules That Know What's Due

Build reusable maintenance schedules by distance, by time, or both — whichever comes first — and apply them to any of your vehicles. Due and coming-up tasks are worked out from your own records, and editing a schedule updates every vehicle it's on.

⚠️

Issues, Tracked Until They're Fixed

Note a noise or a warning light, give it a severity, and keep a troubleshooting timeline of everything you tried. When you find the fix, resolving the issue turns it straight into a service record.

⛽

Fuel & Car Payments

Log fill-ups for real fuel economy measured tank to tank, with partial fills handled properly. Track an optional car loan to see its balance, the next payment due and when it'll be paid off, with a reminder before each due date.

📄

A PDF for a Buyer or a Shop

Create a service history report for any vehicle — every record in date order with mileage, work done and parts. Costs and photos are left out unless you include them, and the whole thing is made on your phone.

🔒

Offline & Private

No account, no sync, no analytics, and no internet permission. Reminders for time-based tasks and a monthly odometer check-in are generated on the device, and JSON backups — photos optional — go only where you send them.
