SoulframeTools

Your Soulframe companion. Browse weapons, armour, and equipment — track your progress, plan your build, and calculate stats without leaving the browser.

Open SoulframeTools
Live at soulframetools.com
Features

What SoulframeTools does

⚔️

Weapons Database

Browse every weapon with stats, scaling, and details. Filter and compare to find the right fit for your build.

🛡️

Armour & Equipment

Full armour set listings with defensive values and set bonuses. Plan your loadout before you grind for it.

⚖️

Weapon Compare

Put weapons side by side and read the differences straight off the table, instead of holding two stat blocks in your head.

🎲

Loadout Randomizer

Generate a random loadout when you want the game to surprise you. Narrow it to the gear you actually own before you roll.

✅

Progress Tracker

Check off items as you acquire them. Track completion across weapons, armour, talismans, and totems in one place.

📐

Build Planner

Allocate virtues and see the numbers move — attack, armour, stability, total life — with tempers, joineries, and pact art bonuses folded in. Import a build or reset to defaults as you experiment.
