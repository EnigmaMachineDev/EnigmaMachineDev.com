Android · Paycheck Allocation

Steward

Write down how you split a paycheck once, and Steward turns every payday into a checklist of exact transfers: "Move $250.00 from Checking to Gifts." You make each transfer in your own bank's app. Steward never moves money, and your data stays on your phone.

Coming to Google Play Privacy Policy
Features

What Steward does

🧾

Rules, In Order

Fixed amounts, a percentage of the paycheck or of what's left, a top-up that brings an account to a target balance, and "everything left" last. Round to the dollar, run a rule only on the second paycheck of the month, or only on bigger paychecks.

👀

See It Before Payday

A live preview shows the exact checklist a paycheck would produce while you edit your rules, including any rule the paycheck can't fully cover.

✅

A Checklist Every Payday

Each paycheck becomes a checklist: what to move, from where, to where. Tick transfers off as you make them, skip one when you need to, and see what stays in the deposit account.

🔗

Bring Your Own SimpleFIN

Connect your own SimpleFIN Bridge and Steward notices when a paycheck lands, makes its checklist, and checks transfers off when they show up. No Bridge? Manual mode does everything by hand, with no connection at all.

🔔

Gentle Reminders

An optional notification when a new checklist is ready, and one reminder if transfers are still open a couple of days later.

🔒

Private By Design

No account, no analytics, no ads, no servers. Your data stays on your phone, and if you connect SimpleFIN, Steward talks to your Bridge and nothing else.
