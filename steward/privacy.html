Steward

Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 13, 2026 App: Steward (Android) Developer: EnigmaMachineDev

Overview

Steward is an Android application that turns each paycheck into a checklist of transfers, from rules you write. It never moves money. Everything you enter is stored on your device. The developer collects nothing: there is no account, no analytics, no crash reporting, no advertising, and no EnigmaMachineDev server.

Steward can be used entirely offline. If you choose to connect a SimpleFIN Bridge, a separate service you sign up for yourself, Steward exchanges data with that Bridge and with nothing else. That is the only network communication the app ever makes.

Information We Collect

The developer collects no information about you. Steward stores the following on your device, in a private application folder:

  • Your accounts: the nicknames and roles you give them, and an optional link to your bank's app or website
  • Your income sources and allocation rules
  • Your paychecks and their checklists, including which transfers you marked done or skipped
  • Your settings

If you connect a SimpleFIN Bridge, Steward also stores on your device the account names, balances and recent transactions the Bridge provides (kept for a rolling window of up to 90 days, 60 by default), and the private access address the Bridge issues to the app.

SimpleFIN Bridge

Connecting is optional. To connect, you create a setup token in your own SimpleFIN Bridge account and paste it into Steward. Steward then:

  • Sends the setup token's claim request to the Bridge once, receiving a private access address in return.
  • Stores that access address only in your phone's secure storage (the Android Keystore). It is never shown, copied, logged, or included in exports.
  • Requests your accounts and recent transactions from the Bridge: when you open the app (at most every few hours), when you pull to refresh, and sometimes in the background when Android allows it. Steward limits itself to 20 requests a day.

All communication with the Bridge uses encrypted HTTPS, and Steward refuses unencrypted addresses. Your financial data travels only between your phone and the Bridge you chose. It never passes through the developer. SimpleFIN Bridge's own handling of your data is governed by its own terms and privacy policy.

How We Use Your Data

Data stored by Steward is used solely to operate the app's features on your device:

  • Recognizing when a paycheck lands and making its checklist
  • Applying your rules, including top-ups that need an account's balance
  • Checking off transfers that appear in your synced transactions
  • Scheduling the notifications you turn on

Notifications

Steward can show a local notification when a new checklist is ready and remind you once about transfers still open. Both are generated and delivered on your device by Android; no push service is used. A notification can include an income source's name and a paycheck amount, so what appears on your lock screen depends on your phone's lock screen settings. Either can be turned off in Settings.

Export & Import

You can export your setup and history as a JSON file through Android's share sheet, and import one through the system file picker. Exports never contain your SimpleFIN access, your transactions or your account balances. Before any import changes your data, Steward saves a copy of what was there on your device so you can restore it. All file operations are local; you choose where exported files go.

Permissions

  • Internet: used only to reach the SimpleFIN Bridge you connect. In manual mode it is never used.
  • Notifications: asked for when you finish setup or turn a notification on.
  • Background work: an optional periodic sync while you are connected to a Bridge.

Steward does not request your contacts, location, camera, microphone or photos. The released app is built without storage permissions and without permission to draw over other apps.

Data Storage & Security

App data is stored in a private folder that belongs to Steward and is not accessible to other apps. The SimpleFIN access address is held in the Android Keystore. Steward turns off Android's app backup, so its data is not copied to your Google account; the export feature is the way to keep a copy. A restored or new phone needs a new setup token to reconnect. Steward has no app lock, so anyone who can open your unlocked phone can open it.

Data Sharing

Steward does not share any data with the developer or with third parties. There are no analytics SDKs, crash reporting services, advertising networks, or developer servers. The only party Steward communicates with is the SimpleFIN Bridge you connect yourself.

Deleting Your Data

Disconnect (Settings → SimpleFIN connection) deletes Steward's access to your Bridge and can also clear synced balances and transactions. Erase all data (Settings) deletes everything Steward holds, including its access and its saved copies. You can also revoke Steward's access from within your SimpleFIN Bridge account, clear the app's data in Android settings, or uninstall the app.

Not Financial Advice

Steward is a planning aid. It does not give financial advice and does not move money.

Children's Privacy

Steward does not knowingly collect any information from children. The developer collects no personal information from any user, regardless of age.
